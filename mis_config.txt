========================================

[HOW TO IDENTIFY]

🌐 MISCONFIGURED CORS
========================================

[HOW TO IDENTIFY]
- Response header:
  Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *

----------------------------------------

----------------------------------------

----------------------------------------
